## Searchable English notes

**English title:** "I Exist Infinitely Many Times"

*This page contains English notes on the German interview, not a verbatim translation.*

Deutsch argues that our universe is one of indefinitely many parallel universes described by quantum theory. The multiplicity is not added to the mathematics. It is his account of what the mathematics says exists and of what produces interference experiments.

The interview presses the apparent absurdity of there being many versions of the same person. Deutsch separates everyday personal identity from the physical fact that quantum systems evolve into histories that largely stop interacting. Most branches cease to interfere at macroscopic scales, which is why people experience one history even though the theory describes more.

He criticizes physicists who use quantum mechanics only as a prediction procedure. Accurate probabilities do not explain which physical processes produce the results. For Deutsch, refusing to discuss objective reality leaves the central scientific question unanswered.
