## About this radio discussion

Martin Rees and Jennifer Nagel argue that human bodies, lifespans, and cognitive machinery place limits on what people can know. Deutsch rejects a fundamental limit on interesting knowledge and distinguishes temporary human constraints from problems that cannot be solved in principle.
