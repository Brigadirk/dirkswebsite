## Searchable English notes

*This page contains English notes, not a verbatim reproduction.*

News reports revived Einstein's cosmological constant after astronomers found that the expansion of the universe is accelerating. Deutsch argues that judging Einstein by whether one discarded equation later resembles dark energy misses the important part of his work.

Einstein insisted that physics describe an objective world which exists independently of observation and explains experience. A prediction can be correct by accident, or for the wrong reason. Scientific understanding requires an account of what exists, how it behaves, and why the observed result follows.

Deutsch contrasts this realism with instrumentalism, the view that a theory need only generate successful predictions. He treats Einstein's resistance to orthodox quantum mechanics as an unfinished attempt to preserve explanation rather than a simple refusal to accept quantum evidence. Einstein's lasting example is the demand for intelligible physical reality, even when a particular theory or objection turns out to be wrong.
