## Searchable English notes

**English title:** "The World Is Bizarre"

*This page contains English notes on the German interview, not a verbatim translation.*

Rafaela von Bredow and Johann Grolle interview Deutsch about parallel universes, time travel, quantum theory, and quantum computers. Deutsch presents the multiverse as the physical reality described by quantum mechanics rather than an optional story placed on top of its equations.

The discussion asks why a theory with such strange consequences should be believed, how interference connects otherwise separate universes, and whether time travel is compatible with physical law. Deutsch distinguishes paradoxes created by a single-history picture from the multiple histories allowed by quantum theory.

He also attacks the twentieth-century habit of treating successful prediction as sufficient while declining to say what exists. In his view, this retreat from objective reality was a scandal inside physics, not a lesson forced on physics by experiment.
