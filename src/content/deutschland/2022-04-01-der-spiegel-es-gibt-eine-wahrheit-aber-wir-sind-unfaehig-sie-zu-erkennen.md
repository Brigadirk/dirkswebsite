## Searchable English notes

**English title:** "There Is a Truth, but We Are Incapable of Recognizing It"

*This page contains English notes on the German interview, not a verbatim translation.*

Johann Grolle and Rafaela von Bredow ask Deutsch about objective truth and the limitless growth of knowledge. People can approach truth through criticism while remaining unable to certify that they possess it. Fallibility can coexist with realism and progress.

The interview applies this view to climate change, technological risk, space travel, and the future of civilization. Deutsch rejects predictions that treat current knowledge or technology as a permanent boundary. A problem becomes soluble when people create the missing explanation and means of acting on it.

Optimism here is not a forecast that events will improve on their own. It is the claim that failures have causes which can be understood and changed. Pessimism becomes dangerous when it recasts present ignorance as a law of nature and discourages the criticism, experiments, institutions, and invention needed to solve the problem.
