They reach the same move by completely different methods. So I'm not surprised AlphaGo destroyed the world champion at Go. I think it did so by the same method used in chess, except that chess isn't such a uniform game. If you say h4 is a much better move than most chess players think, that obviously won't have much generality. You can use ordinary heuristics to think about when it will be good. Go is more uniform: a position occurring here can be extremely analogous to one a dozen squares away. So I'm not that impressed—or, let me put it this way, I'm not GI-type impressed by it.
