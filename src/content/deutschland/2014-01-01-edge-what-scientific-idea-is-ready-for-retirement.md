## Searchable English notes

*This page contains English notes, not a verbatim reproduction.*

Deutsch nominates the "quantum jump" for retirement. Quantum systems have distinguishable states, but quantum change remains continuous in space and time. An electron does not vanish from one orbit and appear in another without passing through a physical process. During tunnelling it has a range of positions and energies rather than one sharply defined classical position.

Calling it a jump hides the unexplained part. Information cannot appear on the far side of a barrier without a physical process carrying the relevant distinction between an electron, proton, or anything else.

Deutsch finds the same mistake in political revolution, Thomas Kuhn's picture of scientific paradigms, and biological saltation. Institutions and adaptations contain knowledge about how to meet problems. Sweeping away one system cannot make the required replacement knowledge appear from nowhere. Variation, criticism, testing, and selection must create it through intermediate steps. In each field, the miraculous jump marks a gap where an explanation should be.
