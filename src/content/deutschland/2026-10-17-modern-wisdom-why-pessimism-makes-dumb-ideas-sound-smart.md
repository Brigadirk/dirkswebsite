The computation going on in a chess engine is a radically different kind of computation from the computation going on in the grandmaster. The grandmaster is doing only about a millionth of the amount of computation that the chess engine does, and yet can reach a very high level. Not quite as high as the program, but still. So there are different types of program. We do not know how to write the grandmaster type of chess program, let alone an AGI. You know, when the grandmaster gets up, he then starts performing computations of orders of magnitude greater complexity than playing chess. What is creativity, in your opinion? How do you think about explaining that simply?

### David Deutsch

<span class="transcript-timestamp">00:44:14 - 00:48:05</span>

### David Deutsch
